# Rules
## Gameplay
1. League setup

A league has 6 to 8 players.
All countries are placed into 3 tiers:

Tier 1: Big Five, Host, and the top projected favorites
Tier 2: the next strongest group
Tier 3: all remaining countries

Tier 1 countries are the safest scoring assets. Tier 2 are balanced picks. Tier 3 are risky upside picks.

Token values can be modified. Suggested values are:
Gold: +5 / -0
Silver: +50 / -25
Bronze: +250 / -125

2. Draft 1: before Semi-Final 1

Each player drafts 2 countries in reverse snake order.

Players can draft from any tier at any time.
Ownership is exclusive, so once a country is drafted, nobody else can take it unless it is traded.

3. Semi-Final 1

Semi-Final 1 countries either qualify or are eliminated.

If a drafted country qualifies, it stays alive and mints a token:

Tier 1 → Bronze
Tier 2 → Silver
Tier 3 → Gold

Tier 1 countries that were already guaranteed finalists still mint Bronze. This keeps the token system simple and consistent.

4. Draft 2: before Semi-Final 2

Each player drafts 2 more countries, again from any tier.

By the end of Draft 2, each player owns 4 countries total.

5. Semi-Final 2

Semi-Final 2 countries either qualify or are eliminated.

Any drafted country that qualifies mints a token based on its tier:

Tier 1 → Bronze
Tier 2 → Silver
Tier 3 → Gold

Tokens belong to the player who owns the country at the moment it qualifies.

6. Trading window

Countries and tokens can both be traded throughout the week.

Trading stays open until final roster lock.

Before a token is minted, trading the country also transfers the right to that future token.
After a token is minted, the token becomes its own separate tradable asset.

7. Post-semi cleanup

After both semifinals, each player checks how many live countries they still have.

If a player has 4 live countries, they must cut down to 3
If a player has 3 live countries, they are ready
If a player has fewer than 3 live countries, they enter the rescue draft
8. Rescue draft

Players who lost too many countries draft from the pool of unowned finalists until they reach 3 final scoring countries.

Rescue picks can score in the final, but they do not mint tokens.

9. Final roster lock

Before the Grand Final begins, each player locks exactly 3 scoring countries.

After this point, trades close.

10. Token placement

After all Grand Final performances are finished, but before results begin, each player places their Bronze, Silver, and Gold tokens on any finalist. These tokens can be placed on any country, even those belonging to another player.

Tokens can be:

stacked on one country
or split across several countries

Placements stay hidden until everyone locks.

11. Final scoring

A player’s total score comes from:

the score of their 3 locked final countries
plus token bonuses if they placed tokens on the actual winner
minus champion-owner penalties if other players correctly placed tokens on the country they own

Only tokens placed on the actual Eurovision winner activate.

When a token activates:

it gives points to the player who placed it
it also docks points from the player who owns the winning country
12. Winner

The player with the highest total score after final country scoring and token resolution wins the league.
